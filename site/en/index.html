Everything you need to see clearly

Six things Mizan does well, without getting in your way.

Track every transaction

Income, expenses and transfers across all your accounts, in one ledger you control.

Budgets that adapt

Set a limit per category and watch it update live as you spend.

Savings goals

Set a target and see a forecast of when you will actually reach it, based on how you save today.

Subscription watch

Finds recurring charges you forgot about and shows what they add up to each month.

Reports

Multi-currency

An AI coach that knows your numbers

Ask plain questions about your own spending and get answers grounded in your data, not generic tips.

The coach describes and forecasts based on data you enter. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

  • Ask about your spending

    “How much did I spend on dining in August?” Answered from your transactions.

  • Scan receipts

    Photograph a receipt and Mizan fills in the amount, merchant and category for you to confirm.

  • Weekly review

    A short summary of the week: what went as planned and what did not.

  • Month-end forecast

    An estimate of where you will land by the end of the month at your current pace.

  • Duplicate and unusual charges

    Flags charges that appear twice and spending that is out of the ordinary for you.

  • “Can I afford this?”

    Enter an amount and see what it does to your budgets and goals before you decide.
